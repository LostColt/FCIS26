    Use it with Google Sheets

    Run the dashboard inside your department's Google Sheet. Everyone keeps adding rows to the Sheet the way they already do, and everyone you share the link with sees the same dashboard. The data never leaves your school's Google account.

    1. Make a Google Sheet in your school account with these headings in row 1: Date, Grade, Course, Teacher, Type. To start from the sample, , then in Google Sheets choose File → Import → Upload.
    2. In the Sheet, choose Extensions → Apps Script.
    3. Delete what's in Code.gs and paste this in:
    4. Click + next to Files, choose HTML, name it Index, delete what's there, and paste in this whole dashboard:
    5. Save. Then Deploy → New deployment → type Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: only people in your school. Click Deploy.
    6. Google asks for permission to read your Sheet. That's normal: it's your own script, reading your own Sheet.
    7. Open the web app link and share it with your department. When someone adds a row to the Sheet, it shows up on the dashboard the next time it's opened.
