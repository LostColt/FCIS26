      Save to a Google Sheet

      Right now your notes live only in this browser, on this computer. Put the tracker inside a Google Sheet and it works on any computer you sign into, stays in your school's Google account, and the Sheet becomes your database: one tab of students, one tab of meetings.

      1. Make a new Google Sheet in your school account.
      2. Choose Extensions → Apps Script.
      3. Delete what's in Code.gs and paste this in:
      4. Click + next to Files, choose HTML, name it Index, delete what's there, and paste this whole tracker in:
      5. Save. Then Deploy → New deployment → type Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: only people in your school. Click Deploy.
      6. Google asks for permission. That's normal: you're letting your own script use your own Sheet.
      7. Open the web app link. The tracker now saves to your Sheet. Bookmark it.

      Can't deploy? Your school may limit Apps Script. Ask your tech lead. And follow your school's rules for where student information is kept.
